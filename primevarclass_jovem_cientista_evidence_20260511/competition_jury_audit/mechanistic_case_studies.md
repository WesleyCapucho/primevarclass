- External label: `0`
- PrimeVarClass locked score: `0.6772260718257691`
- AlphaMissense score/class: `0.8965` / `pathogenic`
- Alignment: `discordant_am_pathogenic_for_benign_label`
- Priority: `highest`
- Evidence gap: `needs AlphaMissense/MAVE/structural confirmation`
